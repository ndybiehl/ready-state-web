Why now

Questions

The things people ask first.

Do I need to own a robot already?

No — ideally you call us before. Readiness is far cheaper and cleaner to do ahead of the robot's arrival, so it works on day one instead of becoming a problem to chase later.

Which robots do you support?

We're robot-agnostic. Home humanoids such as Optimus-class machines, service robots in hospitality and senior living, and fleets at depots, garages, and commercial sites, including Cybercab-style deployments.

What does "readiness" actually cover?

High-bandwidth, low-latency Wi-Fi, wired backhaul, Starlink as backhaul or failover, power, physical space and pathways, safe operating zones, and privacy configuration — plus a path for the announced rooftop Starlink Mobile microcell.

Do you install the rooftop Starlink microcell?

Starlink internet, yes, as backhaul or failover. The rooftop Starlink Mobile microcell is the announced piece: a roof unit meant to help signal reach people inside through thick walls. We plan power, mounting, and a path into the network so it can be added when it ships.

Where do you serve?

Colorado's mountain communities and the Front Range to start, expanding as demand grows. If you're outside that area and have a project, reach out — we'll tell you honestly whether we can help.

Do you sell the robots?

No. We stay independent on purpose, so the only thing we're optimizing for is making your robot work — not moving a unit.
